Almost there

Scan the second QR code on the panel's screen with your camera. It opens the Wi-Fi setup in your normal browser.

No camera app? Open http://192.168.4.1/setup in your browser and enter the PIN shown on the panel.

Browser cannot open it? Turn off mobile data for a moment, or continue on this page (PIN needed).

This page only appears while your phone is connected to the panel's own Wi-Fi network (PX30-...). It has no internet access.
